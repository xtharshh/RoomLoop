    Design notes

    What the brief left open, what I decided, and why.

    The interesting part of this brief: R1 says a recurring booking is all-or-nothing, R2 says a clash must not block the rest. Both are implemented — R2 is the default, R1 is a per-request policy (conflict_policy: "fail"). A series where every occurrence clashes always fails and saves nothing.

    Constraints, handled

    Trade-offs & what I'd do next

    Try the risky paths. Book a weekly series over an existing one-off to see R2 skipping, then flip the policy to “All-or-nothing” to see the rollback. The Denver rooms cross DST on a different date than Berlin — the series drawer shows the UTC offset changing while the wall clock stays fixed.
